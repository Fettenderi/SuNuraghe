# Su Nuraghe

-- Sardinia, 1759 BC --

It's an evening like any other for our humble shepherd. The sheep have finished grazing, but something feels off...
For some time now, they keep disappearing, and in their place... bloodstains on the grass.
On the hill, the shepherd spots a blinding light. "Could it be connected to my sheep?" he wonders. Curious, he draws closer to the source of the light...
What he discovers there will change his life, one way or another...
